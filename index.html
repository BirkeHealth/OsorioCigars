From our fields to your hands

Osorio began in a single tobacco shed outside Estelí, where our founder rolled cigars for neighbors before selling a single stick commercially.

Fifty years later, the same family still sorts every leaf by hand, and the same recipe still ages in cedar before it reaches a box.
